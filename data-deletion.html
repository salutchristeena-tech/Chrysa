Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 21 November 2026

You can ask Chrysa to delete the information it holds about you or your brand at any time, including any data received from Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, WhatsApp) or Google.

1. Disconnect a service in Chrysa

In Chrysa, open Sources, choose the connected service and select Disconnect. Chrysa revokes its access where the service allows and deletes the stored access token straight away. Figures already synced stay with your brand unless you also ask us to delete them (step 3).

2. Remove Chrysa's access from your accounts

  • Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, find Chrysa and select Remove.
  • Instagram: go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find Chrysa and select Remove.
  • Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Chrysa and choose Remove access.

Menu names can change; if you can't find these options, ask us and we will remove access from our side.

3. Ask us to delete your data

Email hello@chrysa.ai with the subject "Data deletion request", from the email address linked to your Chrysa account, and say what you would like deleted: your personal account, a brand, or data from a specific service.

  • We confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • We send you a confirmation when it is done.

4. Close your Chrysa account

Closing your account deletes your account details within 30 days, and with them your access to every brand. A brand's own data is deleted when its owning organisation asks us to, or when its engagement with Chrysa ends.

What is deleted

Your account details, and for a brand: its audits, findings and evidence, comments and decisions, campaign records, synced data from connected services, drafts, stored page captures, and connection tokens.

What may remain

  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups until they expire, within 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as billing records.
  • A record that the deletion happened: who requested it, when, and how many records were removed. It contains no content.
  • Content already published to your social accounts on your instruction, which you can delete from those platforms.
  • Copies outside Chrysa, such as reports already downloaded or sent to other people, which we cannot recall.

Questions

Email hello@chrysa.ai. See also our Privacy Policy.
