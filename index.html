Questions, answered.

What is in the free AI visibility check?

Chrysa asks AI search three questions real customers ask in your category, shows whether you are named and who is named instead, finds the pages AI search trusts and whether they mention you, and writes your first fix. It is free, needs no card, and is limited to one check per brand.

What is Chrysa?

A marketing mind for your brand. Chrysa listens across search, social and AI answers, tells you what deserves your attention today, drafts the next move in your voice, and shows you what worked.

Does Chrysa post on its own?

Never. Chrysa drafts and you decide. It posts only what you approve, on your own accounts, and it never runs ads or changes budgets.

What does “AI answers” mean?

When people ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for a recommendation, does your brand come up? Chrysa asks those questions for you, shows who appears instead, and drafts the changes that help you get mentioned. Some call this GEO or AEO.

Which tools does it connect to?

Google Analytics, Search Console, Instagram and Facebook, Reddit, SEMrush and Modash, with more on the way. You connect each one on that platform's own sign-in page, so Chrysa never sees your passwords.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

A general chatbot guesses from whatever you paste into it. Chrysa answers from your connected sources and names where every number came from, so you can defend the answer in a meeting.

Where does my data go?

Your workspace stays yours. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models, and you can disconnect any source or ask us to erase your brand at any time.

What does early access include?

A place among the first brands on Chrysa, a founding rate locked for as long as you stay subscribed, and a real say in what we build next. Start with a free AI visibility check; subscriptions are billed monthly or annually.
