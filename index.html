Questions

Asked, and answered.

What is in the free AI visibility check?

Chrysa asks AI search three questions real customers ask in your category, without saying who you are. It shows whether you are named, who is named instead, which pages AI search trusts and whether they mention you, and writes your first fix. It is free, needs no card, and is limited to one check per brand.

What does Chrysa actually check?

Chrysa asks an AI model that searches the live web the questions your customers ask, the way AI assistants answer shoppers today. Every result on your page comes from what that search actually said when Chrysa checked. No guesses.

Does Chrysa change my campaigns?

Only with your approval. As each ad platform opens, Chrysa recommends changes to budgets, bids or targeting and applies them when you approve, or within limits you set yourself. Every change is shown to you and logged. Chrysa never posts on your behalf.

Which channels does Chrysa connect to?

Today Chrysa works from your website and from what AI search says about you. Connections to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Ads, Google Analytics and Search Console are being reviewed by each platform. Each will connect on that platform’s own sign-in page, so Chrysa never sees your passwords.

Where does my data go?

Your workspace stays yours. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models, and you can ask us to erase your brand at any time. Read the privacy policy.

What happens after the free check?

Your report opens on a private page. Keep it free, or subscribe and Chrysa re-checks every week, answers your questions, and drafts every fix for your approval. Monthly or yearly, cancel anytime.
