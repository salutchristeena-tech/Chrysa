Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Chrysa collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Chrysa is a marketing and communications intelligence platform operated by CHRYSA FZ-LLC, a free zone company licensed by the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone Authority, at FDRK2722, Compass Building, Al Shohada Road, Al Hamra Industrial Zone-FZ, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates ("Chrysa", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. Where you connect accounts that belong to a business you work for or represent, we process that business's data on its behalf and according to its instructions.

This policy covers chrysa.ai, the Chrysa application, and the connections you choose to make between Chrysa and other services.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Early-access and enquiry forms: name, work email, company, role, team size, regions and the challenge you describe.
  • Free first reading: your website, name and work email, and if you choose, a competitor's name and whether you want our weekly Pulse email.
  • Your account: your name and email address, and your profile picture if your sign-in provider supplies one, when you sign in with Google, LinkedIn or email.
  • Brand details: the brand's website address, category and market, provided by you or your agency.
  • Content you create in Chrysa: comments, notes, decisions and approvals, and drafts of posts or replies you prepare.
  • Billing: your plan and billing contact. Card payments are taken by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

Information we create when we audit a brand

  • Publicly available pages about the brand (its own website and public listings on other sites) that we capture to analyse.
  • The questions we send to AI models, the answers they return, the sources they cite, and the findings we derive.

Information from services you connect

Only when you, or an authorised member of your team, choose to connect a service, and only what that connection's permissions allow. You can disconnect any service at any time.

Technical information

  • Your browser stores your sign-in session so you stay signed in.
  • Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and reliability.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels on chrysa.ai.

3. How we use information

  • To provide Chrysa: run audits, show findings with their evidence, report on campaigns, and support collaboration between your team and your agency.
  • To produce recommendations and campaign plans with our AI features, for you to review and approve.
  • To publish posts, replies or messages to your connected accounts, only when you or your authorised team instruct us to.
  • To operate, secure and improve the service, and to contact you about your account or your request for early access.
  • To prepare and send a free first reading you request.
  • To send the weekly Pulse email, only if you ask for it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use data from connected services for advertising.

  • We never sell, license or rent it, and never share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Google user data

Chrysa's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google Analytics data only to show you and your authorised team your own reports, insights, alerts and campaign plans inside Chrysa.
  • We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • People at Chrysa do not read it, except with your permission, to investigate a security issue or abuse, or to comply with the law.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Chrysa, or remove Chrysa's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. AI processing

Chrysa uses an AI provider, Anthropic, to run audits and to power its AI features. When you use those features, the relevant information, which may include data from services you have connected, is sent to Anthropic to produce the result. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic may not train its models on the content we send through its API. AI-generated content in Chrysa is labelled as such and may be inaccurate; recommendations are for you to review, and nothing is published without your instruction.

10. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by organisation and role and enforced in our database. Connection credentials are stored encrypted and only the service that syncs your data can use them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will tell you as the law requires.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. These rights come from the data protection laws that apply to you, which may include the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and, for people in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR. To use them, email hello@chrysa.ai. We will respond within 30 days.
